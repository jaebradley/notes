# [If you stop writing code, you also get worse at reviewing it](https://cekrem.github.io/posts/if-you-stop-writing-code-you-also-get-worse-at-reviewing-it/)
* Author's analogy is something like
  * Imagine <really good guitarist> says they stopped practicing guitar
  * Instead, they said that they were at an acceptable musical level to maintain and improve their expertise via instructing other guitarists on how to play in
    * General terms ("vibing")
    * Strict musical notation ("spec-driven")
* Author uses example of writing, and specifically an author that
  * Stops writing and only reads and provides feedback to other writes
  * Mostly reads AI-generated prose
* The moment one stop practices problem solving is when one starts getting better at problem solving
* Some lessons are only learned via trial and error, and very few are learned without any effort at all
* Repeated LLM usage for problem solving signals to the brain that if something is hard or requires effort, you shouldn't even try
