# [Someone behind the software](https://karrisaarinen.com/posts/someone-behind-the-software/)
* Two teams can talk to similar customers, have access to the same technology, and make very different things
  * Difference comes from how they understand what they see and the style they go about building the software
* A feature can work perfectly well on its own and still make the product worse
* "Recently, many products are starting to feel messy to me, including products from very successful companies"
  * More thought into what the technology can do with additive product changes vs. thinking about whether the feature improves the core problem
* Easier to add new capabilities vs. rethinking existing capabilities
  * Leads to products becoming a collection of abilities without having a unified point of view
  * Relationship with current work environment of individual ICs working with agents to product (independent) changes
  * In effect, these are individual software factories
