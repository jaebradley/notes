# [It's getting harder to focus every day](https://glyphack.com/attention/)
* Harder and harder for the author to focus, even find an hour to just spend on a single activity
  * Pattern is that they spend 10 minutes on a task and then get distracted
* Author notes that it's almost like their brain finds a way to do something else and avoid painful situations like boredom or hard work
* Author distinguishes between "active" leisure activities, like playing a strategic game (chess) and "passive" leisure activities like browsing on the internet
  * Author makes this distinction because with the rise of blogs, Medium, YouTube, etc, there are more activities to participate in when bored
  * Author ties these outlets when bored to an increased inability to focus on hard things
* Author also finds that they spend a lot of time researching with an LLM vs. starting on the actual project
* Author feels "slow" when interacting with the LLM - author has to wait for a response and correct every responses
  * With the LLM doing X different things, the author has multiple things on their mind and doesn't feel like they can focus
  * This overstimulation using LLMs has another effect on the author which is that doing things without an LLM (and not having that overstimulation) now feels boring - the immediate results don't show up as quickly
