# [Writing as a thinking tool](https://muratbuffalo.blogspot.com/2023/02/writing-as-thinking-tool.html)
* When the author reviews a paper, they think they understand it after reading the paper
  * Then, when the author sits down to write the review, they realize they only understood a portion of the topic matter
  * And then, then writing the review, they sometimes realize that there are connected ideas that weren't identified in the paper or by the paper's authors
* Author also uses writing as a memory tool
  * Author rereads their own blog and reconstructs previous knowledge and understanding - their blog has become a form of external memory
  * The thoughts that the author *didn't* write down did not stick well because they were never fully thought out
* Motivation for writing (and potentially other endeavors) has to be intrinsic because the external rewards are often too delayed and too random to steer
  * Writing while no one is watching is also freeing in its own way
