# [Being ambitious and being a dad](https://nicholascharriere.com/blog/being-ambitious-and-being-a-dad/)
* Turns out, you have a lot of time when you don’t have dependents
* Most great founders are horrible parents
* Author states that many ambitious parents are willing to make a choice that the author is not - to delegate the parenting away
* Author does not believe in the quality time theory but instead believes in maximizing the quantity of time spent with them
  * To the author, there are no diminishing returns to parenting
* Author finds themselves comparing their (work) accomplishments as a parent vs. before kids
* Author feels that life is 10x shorter with kids
* Be ambitious enough to be an ambitious dad

* You can't have it all. There's no perfect life and you can't optimize everything such that you achieve the hypothesized balance between all things competing for your time and energy.
  * Most people at the end of their life would choose more time with the people they care about than more time working even if at that time, they felt they were doing a good job balancing the two.
* [Those who are professionally ambitious and maximizing professional success while being highly confident they are also succeeding at home often have an inaccurate view of their home life](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49353498)
  * "Anyone who thinks they are a great parent, probably isn't."
  * Almost all kids would be happier with a less wealthy upbringing, but a tighter family unit
